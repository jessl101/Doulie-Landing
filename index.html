Will my baby sleep through the night?

Every baby is different, and we won't promise a specific outcome. Doulie is built to help you work toward calmer, more consistent nights with practical, gentle guidance — not guarantees.

Is Doulie medical advice?

No. Doulie offers educational sleep support for caregivers. For medical questions about your baby's health, feeding, or sleep safety, please speak with your pediatrician.

You log feeds, naps, and wake-ups in seconds. Doulie reads those patterns, suggests the next likely window and a calm thing to try tonight, and answers questions in plain language — keeping your baby's context so guidance stays consistent.
