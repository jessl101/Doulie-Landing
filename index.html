A few quick answers.

When can I download Doulie?

Doulie is coming soon to the Apple App Store. Save your spot above and we'll email you the moment it's available. No spam, just the one note.

Will my baby sleep through the night?

Every baby is different, and we won't promise a specific outcome. Doulie is built to help you work toward calmer, more consistent nights with practical, gentle guidance. Progress, not guarantees.

Is Doulie medical advice?

No. Doulie offers educational sleep support for caregivers. For medical questions about your baby's health, feeding or sleep safety, please speak with your pediatrician.

Can my partner use it too?

Yes. Doulie is built for households. Invite your partner or a grandparent with a short code from Settings, and you both log to the same record from your own phones. Either of you can stop sharing at any time.

What does Doulie actually do?

You log feeds, naps and wake-ups in seconds. Doulie reads those patterns, suggests the next likely window and a calm thing to try tonight, and answers questions in plain language. It keeps your baby's context so guidance stays consistent, and you can share the log with a partner so you're both working from the same night.

Doulie starts with a 7-day free trial of personalized AI guidance, then you choose a monthly or annual plan. Exact pricing lands with the App Store release, and the launch email will have it.
